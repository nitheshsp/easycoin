The Primary Account Holder certifies that secondary circle members are family minors aged between 5 and 17 years old. The senior/parent acts as the authorized financial sponsor under RBI Delegated Payment frameworks.

All transactions initiated by the minor under Pre-Approved Limits or Explicit Approvals are debited directly from the primary account. The primary account holder assumes legal liability for authorized transactions.
3. NPCI Monthly Allowance Limits & Transaction Caps
In accordance with NPCI circular guidelines, the maximum cumulative spending limit cannot exceed ₹15,000 per month per minor. The primary user sets customized lower thresholds (e.g., ₹2,000/month) and per-transaction caps (e.g., max ₹500/transaction).
4. Merchant Category Code (MCC) Safety Protection
EasyCoin automatically enforces age-appropriate merchant category filters. Minors are strictly prohibited and technically blocked from spending at gaming/gambling, cryptocurrency, adult entertainment, liquor, tobacco, and high-risk speculative merchants.
5. Instant 1-Tap Revocation & Freeze Rights
The primary account holder retains unconditional, real-time authority to modify limits, pause transactions, or permanently revoke secondary minor access with immediate effect.
6. Spoken & SMS Transaction Audits
Both the primary elder and secondary minor receive real-time notifications with merchant name, amount, timestamp, and remaining allowance upon completion of any payment.
